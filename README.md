# 📰 Advanced News Hub  

## 🌟 Overview  
Advanced News Hub is a **feature-rich desktop application** that aggregates, categorizes, and presents news from **15+ global sources** in a sleek, user-friendly interface. Built with **Python & Tkinter**, it offers **AI-powered summaries, text-to-speech, translations, and personalized favorites**—all in one place!  

---

## 🔥 Key Features  

### 📡 **Multi-Source News Aggregation**  
- Fetches headlines from **BBC, CNN, Reuters, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, and Indian outlets** (The Hindu, NDTV, Times of India, etc.)  

### 🗂️ **Smart Categorization**  
- Auto-sorts articles into **9 categories** (Politics 🏛️, Technology 💻, Sports ⚽, Business 💼, etc.)  

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Summaries**  
- Uses **OpenRouter AI** to generate concise, high-quality summaries  

### 🌍 **Multi-Language Support**  
- Translates articles to **9 languages** (English 🇬🇧, Hindi 🇮🇳, Spanish 🇪🇸, French 🇫🇷, etc.)  
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** 🔊 reads articles aloud in selected languages  

### ⭐ **Personalized Experience**  
- **Save favorites** for quick access  
- **Dark/Light mode** toggle (Ctrl+D)  

### 📊 **Analytics Dashboard**  
- Tracks trends by **category, source, and keywords**  

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack  
- **GUI**: Tkinter (modern styling)  
- **APIs**: OpenWeatherMap (🌤️ weather), OpenRouter (🤖 AI), Google Translator (🌍)  
- **Libraries**: `feedparser`, `gTTS`, `pygame`, `requests`, `PIL`, `langdetect`  

---

## 🚀 How to Use  
1. **Browse** articles with filters (category/source)  
2. **Double-click** to open in browser  
3. **Right-click** for quick actions (summarize, favorite, etc.)  
4. **Use the analytics panel** to track news trends  

---

## ⚙️ Requirements  
- **Python 3.x**  
- Dependencies: See `requirements.txt`  

> **Note:** Requires **API keys** for OpenWeatherMap & OpenRouter.
